Off by default: on mice where the scroll wheel is also the middle-click button, pressing it can wobble the wheel slightly — with this on, that would cancel autoscroll the instant it starts.

Middle-click empty page content to arm autoscroll, then move the pointer to scroll — the farther from where you clicked, the faster it scrolls. Click any button or press Esc to stop. Pressing and dragging (rather than a quick click) stops as soon as you let go.
